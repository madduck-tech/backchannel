Transcript view

Three treatments of the same recording. Left is the other participants, right is you; nothing is labelled, the side is the label. A new turn starts when the side changes or after a pause over 3 s, so consecutive lines from one side read as one turn. Timestamps are the turn start.

1

Messenger

Rounded bubbles; your side takes the brand tint. The most familiar reading.

2

Document

No bubbles. The anchor and a hairline rule carry the side; the transcript reads as a document.

3

Asymmetric

The other side is a card, your side is quiet text. Reads as listening to them.
