Transcript width, corrected: one cap, not two

Issue #118. Variant 1 is approved. Review found two faults in it: its captions were computed against the fallback font, and at the 720 px minimum, with the rail expanded to 288 px, a 60ch cap is wider than the content box, so both sides land on the same edge and the only label the design has is gone. The cap is now the smaller of two, max-width: min(60ch, 78%): the character half stops a 1920 px window stretching the line, the per-cent half keeps the sides apart when the pane is small. Everything else stands: time inside the bubble on the trailing edge of the last line, 6 px between turns, sides at the pane edges, no prose measure.
